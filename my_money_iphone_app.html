Backup & restore

Export a JSON backup regularly and keep it somewhere safe. The backup includes your accounts, transactions, categories, and exchange rates. Do not share it: it contains private financial information.

Data is stored in this browser on this device. Clearing Safari website data or changing the app's website address may make it unavailable. Export backups before deleting or moving anything.
